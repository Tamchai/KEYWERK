Project Background and Significance
KEYWERK was created to address the difficulty of buying mechanical keyboard products through conventional online stores, where customers often cannot clearly compare variations such as color, switch type, compatibility, availability, and product images before making a decision. These limitations can make product selection confusing and may result in customers choosing an unavailable or unsuitable configuration.

The project provides a specialized shopping experience in which customers can explore product variants, check stock for each configuration, manage their cart, complete a secure checkout, and follow their order from payment to delivery. It also gives store administrators a centralized workspace for managing products, variants, inventory, payments, and order fulfillment. Beyond serving as an online storefront, KEYWERK demonstrates how a complete e-commerce workflow can maintain accurate inventory and payment states while providing a clear experience for both customers and store operators.
